# Product Requirements — NOMAD Music

## Product

Spotify-inspired music-first UX + Apple-style editorial presentation + YouTube-scale discovery + personal/local music + a recommendation/Vibe engine.

## Initial surfaces

- Home
- Search
- Discover
- Library
- Playlists
- Now Playing
- Lyrics
- AI / Vibe
- Settings

## Signature features

- Canonical Track Graph
- Unified provider identity
- Vibe Match
- Why This recommendation explanations
- Smart Radio
- Vibe Journey
- Playlist DNA
- Playlist Doctor
- AI Playlist Builder
- AI DJ / queue control

## Non-goals

- Do not build a giant mirrored commercial music archive.
- Do not make an LLM the recommendation engine.
- Do not make the first release depend on a GPU or expensive infrastructure.
